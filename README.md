# Free AI Trivia Night Pack

A print-and-play trivia pack all about artificial intelligence — friendly, plain-English,
and completely screen-free for players. Built by [Asdale Dynamics, LLC](https://dylanasdale.github.io),
who explain AI like a friend would.

## What's inside

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `trivia-night-pack.html` | The full printable pack: cover, hosting tips, eight written rounds with answers, tiebreakers, and team answer sheets. Print it and you're ready to host. |
| `answer-key.html` | A standalone one-page HTML answer key for the host or grader. |

## The eight rounds

1. **Influential Moments** — six landmark moments in AI history
2. **Common Myths** — true-or-false misconceptions, gently corrected
3. **How It Works** — plain-English questions on prompts, hallucinations, and more
4. **Staying Safe** — deepfakes, voice cloning, phishing, and good habits
5. **AI in Everyday Life** — the AI everyone already uses without noticing
6. **Famous Names** — the companies and models behind the chatbots
7. **Pop Culture AI** — HAL 9000 to WALL·E
8. **The Mixed Bag** — a little of everything to finish the night

Each round has six questions (48 points total), plus three nearest-guess tiebreakers.

## How to use it

No installation, no screens for players, no tracking — it's paper.

1. Open `trivia-night-pack.html` in any browser.
2. Press **Ctrl/Cmd + P** to print. Each section is designed to fit its own US Letter or A4 page.
3. Print one full pack for the host and one answer-sheets page per team (plus a few spares).
4. Keep `answer-key.html` for scoring, or rely on the answers printed on each host sheet.

Questions are read aloud twice; teams write answers on the printed sheets or scrap paper.
Score between rounds, most points after Round 8 wins, tiebreakers settle close calls.

## Hosting notes

Full tips are printed inside the pack (page 2), tuned for libraries and classrooms:
pencils over pens, grade between rounds, accept close answers, and keep the tone warm —
this night is about curiosity, not homework.
